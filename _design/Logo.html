FIG. 01 · APERTURE GEOMETRY · 100 × 100

A scholarly ring contains an asymmetric cluster of three nodes. One is gold.

The outer ring reads as a microscope field or a cell boundary — the scientist's framed observation. Inside, three connected nodes form a small network: nodes for the neural and the molecular, edges for the interactions both share. The gold node sits at the upper-right, the signal the rest of the field is organized around.

The wordmark is set in Cormorant Garamond, drawn after Claude Garamont's 16th-century romans.

It carries the long-sentence rhythm of an academic press without feeling antique. The italic does the work of emphasis on the surname — softer, slightly forward-leaning, a single moment of motion in an otherwise still composition.
